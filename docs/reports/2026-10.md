## Redmond publishes proposed permanent drone land-use regulations

2026-10-21 — Redmond. The city posted draft permanent regulations for Planning Commission review beginning October 28, with a November 18 public hearing. The draft is not an adopted final ordinance; interim controls remain the existing framework.

Status: Draft regulations; hearings scheduled.

Source: [City of Redmond](https://www.redmond.gov/2538/Drone-Port-Land-Uses)

## Westborough approves drone bylaw; state review remains pending

2026-10-19 — Westborough. Town Meeting approved the Unmanned Aircraft General Bylaw 510–13 on October 19. It was submitted to the Attorney General October 22; approval from both the Attorney General and MassDOT is required before effectiveness. A zoning appeal is scheduled November 2 and the site-plan hearing was continued to November 10.

Status: Town-approved; state approvals required.

Source: [Town of Westborough](https://www.westboroughma.gov/1541/Prime-Air-Drone-Delivery-Service-Applica)

## Congressional delegation requests site-specific environmental review

Status: Environmental assessment requested.

Source: [Town of Westborough](https://www.westboroughma.gov/1541/Prime-Air-Drone-Delivery-Service-Applica)
